# 100 Days of Code in C: Questions

Question list for the UPES 100 Days of Code challenge (source: https://maitysoumik.github.io/basic-coding-with-c/).

- Days 1 to 50: two problems a day (Q1 to Q100)
- Days 51 to 100: one problem a day (Q101 to Q150)

## Contents

| Days | Questions |
|------|-----------|
| 1 to 50 | Q1 to Q100 |
| 51 to 100 | Q101 to Q150 |
